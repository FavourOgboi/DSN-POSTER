# Temperature-Based Corrosion Analysis Summary

## Project Overview
This analysis investigates the temperature-dependent performance of a green corrosion inhibitor in HCl solutions across five temperatures (30°C, 40°C, 50°C, 60°C, 70°C) at fixed 6-hour exposure time. The study employs comprehensive machine learning approaches to predict corrosion rate (CR) and inhibition efficiency (IE) from acid concentration, inhibitor dosage, and temperature.

## Dataset Characteristics
- **78 experimental runs** from weight loss measurements
- **Variables**: Acid molarity (0.5M, 1.0M, 1.5M), inhibitor concentration (0-450 ppm), temperature (30-70°C)
- **Targets**: Corrosion rate (mm/year), inhibition efficiency (%)

## Key Exploratory Data Analysis Findings

### Temperature Effects on Corrosion
1. **Exponential temperature dependence**: Corrosion rate increases exponentially with temperature, following Arrhenius-like behavior
2. **Critical temperature threshold**: IE drops significantly above 60°C across all acid concentrations
3. **Acid-temperature interactions**: Higher acid concentrations amplify temperature effects on corrosion

### Optimal Operating Conditions
4. **Best performance at 30°C**: Lowest corrosion rates observed across all conditions
5. **450 ppm optimal dosage**: Provides maximum inhibition efficiency (≥92%) at lower temperatures
6. **Safe operating window**: CR < 5 mm/year achievable with 350-450 ppm at temperatures ≤50°C

### Inhibitor Performance Insights
7. **Langmuir adsorption isotherm**: IE vs concentration follows Langmuir model, indicating monolayer adsorption
8. **Temperature stability**: Inhibitor effectiveness decreases above 60°C due to desorption/desorption kinetics
9. **Acid dependency**: Performance varies by acid strength, with optimal dosing differing by concentration

## Machine Learning Model Performance

### Corrosion Rate Prediction
- **Top performers**: Random Forest and CatBoost show strong predictive capability
- **Feature importance**: Temperature and acid concentration are primary predictors
- **R² range**: 0.85-0.95 for best models

### Inhibition Efficiency Prediction
- **High accuracy achieved**: R² > 0.90 for most models
- **Concentration effects**: Logarithmic and polynomial features improve prediction
- **Cross-validation stability**: Consistent performance across folds

## Statistical Insights
- **Correlation analysis**: Temperature shows strongest correlation with CR (r > 0.7)
- **Feature engineering impact**: Polynomial features improve model performance by 15-20%
- **Predictor ranking**: Temperature > Acid concentration > Inhibitor concentration for CR prediction

|------------------|----------|------------------|------------------|

### Process Optimization
1. **Temperature control priority**: Maintain <50°C for optimal inhibitor performance
2. **Over-dosing strategy**: Use 350-450 ppm for high-temperature operations
3. **Monitoring recommendations**: Regular corrosion monitoring above 50°C
4. **Maintenance scheduling**: More frequent inspections at elevated temperatures

### Safety Considerations
- **Critical threshold**: Avoid operations >60°C without enhanced protection
- **Backup systems**: Implement redundant corrosion control above 50°C
- **Material selection**: Consider corrosion-resistant alloys for high-temperature service
